Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about StudyBolt.

How do I turn a PowerPoint into a study set?

Open StudyBolt, select the import or upload option, choose a supported PowerPoint file, and follow the on-screen steps. Once processing finishes, review your generated study resources.

My PowerPoint or PDF won't upload. What should I do?

Check your internet connection, confirm the file format is supported, and try a smaller file. Restart the app and retry. If the issue continues, email support with the error message and file size.

How do flashcards and quizzes work?

StudyBolt generates review materials from imported course content. Open your study set to access flashcards and practice questions. Check generated answers against your original lecture materials.

What is StudyCast?

StudyCast is StudyBolt's audio study experience for listening to supported study materials. Availability may depend on the material, app version, and subscription.

What if AI-generated notes are inaccurate?

AI-generated materials can contain errors or missing information. Verify important facts against your lecture slides, readings, and instructor guidance. Report recurring problems to our support team.

How do I restore a subscription?

Open StudyBolt's subscription or account screen and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account as the original purchase. If your access remains unavailable, contact support.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, select Subscriptions, choose StudyBolt, and select Cancel Subscription if available. Deleting StudyBolt does not cancel a subscription. Apple's cancellation guide .

How can I request a refund?

For purchases billed through Apple, visit reportaproblem.apple.com . Refund eligibility and decisions are handled by Apple. You can also email StudyBolt support for assistance understanding a charge.

I'm having trouble signing in. What should I do?

Check your connection and use the same sign-in method as before. Restart StudyBolt and retry. For Apple sign-in, use the Apple Account originally associated with StudyBolt. Contact support if problems continue.

How do I delete my StudyBolt account?

Use the account-deletion option in StudyBolt's account settings if available. If you cannot access the app or need assistance, email lecoffeeconfit@gmail.com . We may verify account ownership before processing your request. Cancel any active Apple subscription separately.

Can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Yes! Email lecoffeeconfit@gmail.com with your feedback. For bugs, include the steps to reproduce the issue and a screenshot if helpful.

Where can I find your privacy policy and terms?

Visit our Privacy Policy for data-handling information and our Terms of Service for the conditions of using StudyBolt.
