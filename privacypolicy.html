01. Overview

Welcome to StudyBolt. StudyBolt is operated by Howard Tran (“StudyBolt,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the StudyBolt app and related services.

StudyBolt helps learners turn study materials into notes, flashcards, quizzes and audio, ask AI Tutor questions, plan study sessions, track progress, and share selected study content.

Study Packs and learning history are stored on your device by default. When you choose a cloud feature, the information needed for that feature is sent to StudyBolt’s processing server and service providers as described below.

This policy describes the current app and server code. It does not promise that every feature is available on every device or account.

03. Study materials and AI

Local storage. StudyBolt saves Study Packs and learning information in the app’s local device storage. This can include imported material, extracted text, notes, quiz answers, plans and progress. Signing out does not automatically remove that local study data.

Cloud document processing. When you ask StudyBolt to process a supported file, the file is sent to StudyBolt’s processing server. PDF and document content is then sent to OpenAI for the requested output. PowerPoint text is extracted on the processing server; selected slide images and related metadata may be saved in private Supabase storage for the visual-review feature. Text notes and AI Tutor requests are sent as request content rather than as a stored StudyBolt file.

AI Tutor. A cloud Tutor request sends your question and the relevant current and nearby Study Pack sections, plus recent Tutor conversation or the selected answer context when needed. It does not send your entire library. On compatible devices, the on-device Tutor processes its prompt on the device. StudyCast uses the device’s text-to-speech voices and does not request microphone access.

Temporary provider files and responses. StudyBolt asks the OpenAI Responses API not to store response application state and requests deletion of uploaded provider files after processing. These controls do not disable OpenAI safety logs or change the OpenAI API project’s data-sharing settings. OpenAI says API data is not used to train models by default unless the API customer opts in; StudyBolt has not verified whether its current API project has opted in. Until that setting is confirmed, StudyBolt cannot promise that API inputs are excluded from model training.

OpenAI’s standard API abuse-monitoring logs may contain prompts or responses and are retained for up to 30 days by default, with longer retention possible for legal or security reasons. See OpenAI’s data controls.

AI-generated notes, explanations and answers can be wrong or incomplete. Review them against your course materials and instructor guidance.

04. How we use information

We use information to provide the features you request, authenticate and secure accounts, process study materials, save local progress, create requested AI outputs, operate sharing and class discovery, respond to support requests, administer Premium access, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Signed-in app activity summaries contain event type and time, duration, screen and aggregate quiz counts. They do not contain study text or individual quiz responses.

StudyBolt does not use study content to train its own AI model. Whether the OpenAI API project shares API data for OpenAI model improvement remains unverified; the current setting must be checked before this policy is treated as finally approved.

05. Sharing and providers

StudyBolt uses service providers only for the functions described here:

  • StudyBolt’s VPS processing server receives files and requests when you use cloud document processing or AI features.
  • Supabase provides authentication, database, server-function and private visual-storage services.
  • OpenAI processes material and prompts for cloud document generation and AI Tutor features.
  • RevenueCat processes subscription status and purchase records using your StudyBolt account identifier.
  • Apple and Google may process sign-in information when you choose their sign-in options and handle app-store purchases, payments and subscription management.
  • Google may process messages you send to our Gmail support address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the published StudyBolt legal pages and may process standard website request logs.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies and, where applicable, our service arrangements with them.

Advertising and sale. StudyBolt does not display third-party ads, sell personal information, or use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. No advertising identifiers or ad-targeting controls are used by the current app.

We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or another person’s rights and safety.

06. Data security

StudyBolt uses account authentication, access controls and private storage rules to protect information, and sends cloud requests over HTTPS. Access to account-linked records is limited by the app’s authenticated server and database rules.

No app, network or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your account credentials and avoid uploading sensitive information that is not needed for your study task.

07. Data retention and deletion

On your device. Local Study Packs and progress remain until you delete or reset them in the app, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

StudyBolt account data. When an account deletion request succeeds, StudyBolt deletes the authentication account and associated account-linked database records, including study activity summaries, support records, sharing records and subscription-event records. The deletion function also removes private PowerPoint visual files from StudyBolt storage. The app clears its local StudyBolt data after the server confirms deletion.

RevenueCat and store records. The current account-deletion function does not send a deletion request for the linked RevenueCat customer record. RevenueCat may therefore retain that purchase/subscription record under its own retention practices. Deleting your StudyBolt account also does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it in the relevant store account.

AI provider records. StudyBolt requests deletion of temporary uploaded files from OpenAI after processing and uses the Responses API with response storage disabled. OpenAI’s standard abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days, or longer where law or security requires. Provider backups, hosting logs and legally required records may remain under the applicable provider settings and retention policies.

StudyBolt does not promise a fixed deletion period for every system backup, provider log or external purchase record. Those periods depend on the system and provider, and may extend beyond account deletion where law or security requires.

To delete your StudyBolt account, open Profile → Account → Delete account. The app reports success only after the server confirms the account deletion.

10. Children’s privacy

StudyBolt is designed for students aged 13 and older and is not intended for children under 13. Do not create an account or use StudyBolt if you are under 13. If you are a minor, obtain parent or guardian permission where the law applicable to you requires it.

The current app does not ask for a date of birth or verify age before account creation. If you believe a person under 13 has provided information to StudyBolt, contact us at lecoffeeconfit@gmail.com so we can review and delete the account and information as required.
