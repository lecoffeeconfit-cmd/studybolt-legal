01. Overview

StudyBolt is operated by Howard Tran ("StudyBolt," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use the StudyBolt mobile application, related websites, and learning services (collectively, the "Service").

StudyBolt helps students organize learning materials, generate study notes, build flashcards, create quizzes, listen to study content, plan study sessions, and track their learning progress.

By using the Service, you acknowledge the information practices described in this policy. Where the law requires consent, we will request it separately.

Our goal is to make learning more useful while giving you understandable choices about your information.

03. Study materials and AI processing

StudyBolt may process materials you choose to upload or create, including PDF files, slide presentations, text notes, and other supported educational content.

Study libraries and uploaded study materials are private by default. Public discovery and link-based sharing are disabled for the initial production release. If sharing becomes available later, it will require a user-initiated action and updated disclosures as necessary.

How study content is used

  • Extracting supported text and study information.
  • Creating notes, summaries, flashcards and quizzes.
  • Generating or preparing audio study content.
  • Saving and displaying materials in your library.
  • Supporting learning progress and study organization.

AI-assisted features

StudyBolt uses automated processing and may transmit the content needed for a requested AI Tutor, summary, flashcard, quiz, or study-audio feature to an AI service provider. Such content may include excerpts from uploaded documents, user prompts, and generated responses. AI processing is separate from publicly sharing your study materials. Where personal data is shared with a third-party AI provider, the app must disclose that processing and obtain permission before sharing where required by applicable law and platform rules. If the app has not yet presented the necessary permission, the affected feature should not transmit that personal data.

You should avoid uploading sensitive personal information, confidential third-party documents, or material you are not authorized to process.

Important: AI outputs may contain errors. Review generated summaries, answers, and study content before relying on them for important decisions.

Model training

StudyBolt uses submitted content to generate the study results you request. Whether an AI provider retains inputs and outputs or uses them to improve its models depends on the provider and the settings of the production integration. We do not represent that all AI inputs are immediately deleted or excluded from model improvement without verification. You may contact us for information about the provider and processing applicable to your request. Do not include confidential or highly sensitive information in AI prompts or uploads.

05. Sharing and service providers

StudyBolt may rely on service providers to operate features such as authentication, file storage, AI processing, diagnostics, and payments.

Categories of recipients may include:

  • Infrastructure and storage providers: hosting application services and storing data.
  • Authentication providers: supporting account creation and sign-in.
  • AI processing providers: processing content for supported AI features, where used.
  • Diagnostics providers: detecting crashes, errors and service issues, where enabled.
  • App stores and payment providers: processing purchases and subscriptions, where applicable.

StudyBolt’s production architecture includes self-hosted Supabase services for authentication and app data, hosted on a Contabo server managed through Coolify; Apple Sign-In for authentication; and Apple App Store and RevenueCat for subscription purchases and entitlement management. These services may receive account identifiers, authentication tokens, stored study information, technical information, or purchase status as necessary for their roles. AI model services may receive study excerpts and prompts when you request AI features. Other sign-in and diagnostic integrations are used only if enabled in the deployed app. See Supabase, Contabo, Apple, and RevenueCat for their published privacy information. Additional providers may be involved in requested AI features or app diagnostics where enabled; we will provide the relevant information and obtain required permissions.

We select service providers based on their roles and applicable terms, and use technical and contractual safeguards where available and appropriate. Providers may process information under their own privacy notices and agreements as well as our instructions.

Sale and advertising-related sharing

StudyBolt is designed as a study service, not an advertising network. We do not presently describe or authorize the sale of users’ uploaded study materials or disclosure of private study sets to advertisers. We do not intentionally make private study libraries publicly available. If we later introduce advertising, cross-context behavioral targeting, or a practice legally considered a sale or sharing of personal information, we will update this policy and provide legally required notice, consent, and opt-out choices before enabling it. Any applicable privacy choices will be provided as required by law.

We may also disclose information when legally required or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or the rights of others, subject to applicable law.

06. Data security

We take the security of account information and educational content seriously. We use safeguards designed to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and loss.

Depending on the relevant system, these may include encryption during transmission, access controls, authenticated sessions, restricted server access, and operational monitoring.

No transmission, server, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their sign-in credentials and keep their devices updated.

If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, contact StudyBolt support using the information at the end of this page.

07. Data retention and deletion

StudyBolt retains information only for periods appropriate to the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal, security, and operational obligations.

Different kinds of information may have different retention periods, including:

  • Account information: while needed to maintain the account, unless otherwise required by law.
  • Study materials and learning records: according to the app's storage and deletion functionality.
  • Diagnostics and security logs: for a limited operational or security period.
  • Transaction records: as necessary for billing, accounting, disputes or legal compliance.

Account records, saved study materials, and learning history are generally maintained while the account or corresponding content remains active. When an account or item is deleted, associated active-service data is scheduled for removal in accordance with the implemented deletion workflow; limited data may remain temporarily in backups, system logs, payment records, or where legal and security obligations require it. AI providers may have separate retention periods. Exact backup, diagnostic-log, and provider retention windows depend on the deployed systems and are not represented here as immediate or fixed-duration deletion.

Deleting your account

You may request deletion of your StudyBolt account and associated personal information through the available in-app account deletion controls or by contacting support.

Account deletion may be subject to limited retention required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, or other lawful purposes.

Use the account deletion control available from your StudyBolt account settings, or email lecoffeeconfit@gmail.com to request help. We may verify that you control the account before acting. We will process verified requests as required by applicable law and inform you if an exception applies. Removing the app from your device does not, by itself, delete a server-side account or cancel an App Store subscription.

Deleting an account does not necessarily cancel an active subscription managed by Apple, Google, or another billing provider. Such subscriptions must be managed through the relevant provider's subscription settings.

10. Children's privacy

StudyBolt is an educational service for students and independent learners, including teenagers. For the current consumer release, StudyBolt is intended for users age 13 and older and is not designed for use by children under 13. Users who are minors must have a parent or guardian review their use of the Service and provide authorization when required by applicable law. In some regions, additional age-related consent rules apply.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this consumer service. If we learn that a child under 13 has supplied personal information, we will review the account, restrict further processing, and delete the information as required by applicable law. Parents and guardians can contact us to request review or deletion. StudyBolt does not currently offer a separate school-managed or parental-consent onboarding program for children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a manner inconsistent with applicable requirements, contact support so the matter can be reviewed.
